# Media

`npm run media` reads originals from `assets-src/media` and writes the files the site serves. A missing original becomes a labeled plate: Anti Black into Carbon, a faint red glow, and the filename in JetBrains Mono. Videos of that kind are 6 seconds. Drop the real file on the same name in `assets-src/media` and run the command again.

Originals over 25 MB are listed in `assets-src/media/.gitignore` by that command and stay off the repo. Smaller originals are committed.

Posters (`*-poster.jpg` and `*-poster.avif`) and `public/media/hero-frames/` are derived. They are not separate Imagine files. Hero posters also include a WebP of the first frame. Scrub films are H.264, 24 fps, no audio, faststart, yuv420p, desktop 1920 by 1080 and phone 1080 by 1920, kept to the source length and trimmed at 10 seconds. The encode starts at CRF 24 with a keyframe on every frame. If a file is over about 6 MB it steps to CRF 26, then 28, then a keyframe every 4 frames, then 1600 wide for desktop. The shipped hero files are CRF 28 with a keyframe every 4 frames, at full size. The canvas fallback is 120 to 180 WebP frames. Door films are WebM AV1 and MP4 H.264, no audio, at most 1080 by 1350. Stills used on the page go through Astro's Picture component at 640, 1280, 1920, and 2560 where the original is that wide. `og.jpg` is the fixed 1200 by 630 social card at `public/og/og.jpg`.

Every Imagine prompt ends with: no text, no logos, photoreal, brand palette of black, bone and one red accent. Every video prompt also ends with: one slow continuous camera move, no cuts, no shake.
