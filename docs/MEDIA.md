# Media

`npm run media` reads originals from `assets-src/media` and writes the files the site serves. A missing original becomes a labeled plate: Anti Black into Carbon, a faint red glow, and the filename in JetBrains Mono. Videos of that kind are 6 seconds. Drop the real file on the same name in `assets-src/media` and run the command again.

Originals over 25 MB are listed in `assets-src/media/.gitignore` by that command and stay off the repo. Smaller originals are committed.

Posters (`*-poster.jpg`, `*-poster.avif`, and `*-poster.webp`) and `public/media/hero-frames/` are derived. They are not separate Imagine files. Scrub films are H.264, 24 fps, no audio, faststart, yuv420p, desktop 1920 by 1080 and phone 1080 by 1920, kept to the source length and trimmed at 10 seconds. The encode starts at CRF 24 with a keyframe on every frame. If a file is over about 6 MB it steps to CRF 26, then 28, then a keyframe every 4 frames, then 1600 wide for desktop. The shipped hero files are CRF 28 with a keyframe every 4 frames, at full size. The canvas fallback is 120 to 180 WebP frames. Hover films (the four doors, the guide cards, the latest drop, and the video cards) are WebM AV1 and MP4 H.264, 24 fps, no audio, trimmed to 4 to 6 seconds. 4:5 films stay at most 864 by 1080. 16:9 films stay at most 1280 by 720. Nothing is upscaled. Each file starts at CRF 26 for H.264 and CRF 34 for AV1, then CRF rises by 2 until the file is under about 1.5 MB. Posters are the first frame as JPEG quality 80, AVIF, and WebP, under about 150 KB. Doors land in `public/media/`. The other hover films land in `public/media/loops/`. A hover film with no source is not encoded: the card keeps its still. Stills used on the page go through Astro's Picture component at 640, 1280, 1920, and 2560 where the original is that wide. `og.jpg` is the fixed 1200 by 630 social card at `public/og/og.jpg`.

Every Imagine prompt ends with: no text, no logos, photoreal, brand palette of black, bone and one red accent. Every video prompt also ends with: one slow continuous camera move, no cuts, no shake.
