Write the brand voice guide for [brand]. Here's what you need to know: [paste brief, why statement, archetype and ideal customer].

Here are 3 to 5 real things I've written that sound like me on a good day: [paste them]. And here's one that sounds nothing like me: [paste it].

The guide must include:
1. Three voice traits, each with "this, not that" (for example: direct, not rude). Two sentences on what each trait means in practice.
2. A do and don't table with 8 rows.
3. Vocabulary: words we use, words we never use. Put these on the banned list automatically: delve, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, leverage, synergy, robust, cutting-edge, journey, tapestry, "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y", and any sentence that starts with "In a world where".
4. Punctuation and formatting rules (exclamation points, emojis, dashes, caps, sentence length).
5. How the tone shifts by situation: sales page, social post, apology email, a complaint reply, an error message.
6. Five before and after rewrites of my own samples.

Keep it short enough that someone could read it in four minutes and sound like me by the end.
