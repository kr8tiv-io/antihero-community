You're a logo designer with 20 years of experience and strong opinions. Create a concept sheet of 10 genuinely different logo ideas for [brand name], a [business type] for [customer]. Personality: [3 traits]. Archetype: [archetype]. Palette: [hex codes]. Fonts: [headline font].

Spread the 10 across types:
- 2 wordmarks (the name set in type, with one custom detail)
- 2 lettermarks (initials)
- 2 symbols or pictorial marks
- 2 combination marks (symbol plus name)
- 1 emblem or badge
- 1 wildcard that breaks a rule on purpose

For each concept: a name, the idea and what it means, a description precise enough that a designer could draw it without asking questions, the font, how color is used, and how it holds up at 32 pixels and in one color.

Then [if your AI can make images]: generate the top 4 as flat vector-style images on a plain white background, centered, no mockups, no extra text. Don't worry if the lettering comes out wrong. Image models still mangle letters, and we'll set the type properly later.

Finish by ranking all 10 on simple, memorable, scalable and right for this brand, with one line on each.
