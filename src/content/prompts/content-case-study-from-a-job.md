---
title: "Case study from a real job"
useItFor: "Turn a finished job into proof that sells the next one."
category: Prompts
goal: content
order: 611
---

Interview me about a job I just finished, one question at a time: the customer (no names unless I say so), the problem, what they tried before, what we did, how long it took, the result in numbers or photos, and what they said.
Then write it three ways: a 150-word website case study, a social post with a before/after hook, and a 2-sentence version for quotes and proposals. Only use what I told you.
