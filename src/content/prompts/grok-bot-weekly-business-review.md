useItFor: "Get a Friday summary of what happened, what slipped and what's next."

Every Friday at [time], go through my email, calendar and [files/notes] from this week and send me a one-page review: what got done, money in and money out that you can see, quotes or leads that went quiet, promises I made that I haven't kept, and the 3 most important things for next week.
Keep it under 300 words. If you're not sure about something, say so instead of guessing. Draft only: never send anything on my behalf.
