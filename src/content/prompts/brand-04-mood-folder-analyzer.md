I'm attaching [number] images from my mood folder: fonts, logos, brands, websites, packaging and photos I like. Analyze them like a design director who doesn't flatter clients.

1. Common threads: colors, contrast, typography, layout density, photography, textures, overall mood. Be specific.
2. Name the aesthetic I'm drawn to in 3 words, and name 2 real brands that already live in that space.
3. Pull a draft palette of 5 to 7 hex codes from the images, and tell me which image each came from.
4. Typography: classify the type I keep picking (geometric sans, grotesk, humanist sans, serif, slab, condensed display, script) and suggest free equivalents from Google Fonts or Fontshare.
5. Photo and image style: lighting, color grade, subjects, how much empty space.
6. Outliers: which images don't fit the rest and why. Ask me whether to keep or cut them.
7. Three distinct directions I could take. For each: a name, two sentences of description, palette, font pair, imagery style, and one brand it reminds you of.

Then ask me which direction feels most like me, and why. Keep in mind my business is [what you do] for [who], and my personality words are [3 words].
