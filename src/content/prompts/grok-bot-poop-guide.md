Make me a funny but genuinely useful PDF guide called "How to Take a Great Poop." 6 to 8 short sections: fibre, water, timing and routine, posture (footstool, lean forward), don't scroll on the toilet, don't strain, and a clear "see a doctor if" box. Keep it tasteful and family-friendly, with dry humour. No medical claims: use soft wording like "can help" and "for a lot of people." Plain words, short lines. Ask me up to 3 questions before you start if you need to. When it's done, send me the PDF file and a picture of the cover.
