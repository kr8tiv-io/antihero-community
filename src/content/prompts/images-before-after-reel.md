I have a before photo and an after photo of [job]. Write me a Grok Imagine prompt for a 6-second vertical video: start on the before, a smooth wipe or reveal to the after, slow push-in, natural lighting, no added text.
Then write a caption with a hook, what we did in one line, and a call to action. Give me 3 caption options.
