useItFor: "Get interviewed, then get a plan on one page that uses only your real numbers."

---

Interview me, then write a one-page business plan for [business]. Ask one question at a time, at least 10 questions: what I sell, who buys it, why they'd pick me over doing nothing, how they find me, what I charge, what it costs me, and what could kill this. Push back on vague answers.
Then write the page with these headings: What we sell, Who it's for, Why us, How people find us, The money, Biggest risks, Next 3 steps. Under The money, use only numbers I gave you and mark anything missing as TODO. No projections I didn't give you.
