useItFor: "Catch the accessibility problems that lock people out of your site."

Audit [URL or pasted HTML] for accessibility against WCAG 2.2 AA, focused on what real visitors hit first: colour contrast, image alt text, heading order, link and button labels, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, form labels and error messages, motion and autoplay.
Give each issue as: where it is, who it affects, the fix (with a code snippet if it helps), and severity. Don't pad the list. If something passes, say so in one line.
