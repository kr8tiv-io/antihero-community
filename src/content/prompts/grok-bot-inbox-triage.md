Go through my unread email from the last [24 hours]. Sort it into: needs me today, can wait, FYI, and junk. For anything in "needs me today", draft a short reply in my voice and tell me what you assumed.
Don't send, delete, archive or unsubscribe from anything. Give me the list with one line per email, then the drafts.
