Here's my brand brief: [paste brief, or describe the business, customer and personality in a few sentences].

Step 1, archetype. From the 12 brand archetypes (Innocent, Everyman, Hero, Outlaw, Explorer, Creator, Ruler, Magician, Lover, Caregiver, Jester, Sage), recommend a primary and a secondary archetype for me. Explain why in plain English. Show how each choice would change my headline, my colors and my photos. Tell me which archetypes my competitors are already using so I don't blend in.

Step 2, positioning. Write 3 versions of this statement:
"For [target customer] who [need or frustration], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe]. Unlike [main alternative], we [real difference]." Mark the one that's most honest and the one that's most bold.

Step 3, ideal customer. Build a profile of one specific person: name, age range, job, a day in their life, what they've already tried, what they're sick of, the exact words they'd type into Google, where they hang out online, and what would make them trust me in 10 seconds. Then list 3 people who are NOT my customer, so I stop trying to please them.
