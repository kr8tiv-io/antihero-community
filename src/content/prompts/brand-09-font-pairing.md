Recommend 5 headline and body font pairings for [brand], a [business type] whose personality is [3 traits] and whose archetype is [archetype]. Free fonts only, from Google Fonts or Fontshare, and include the link to each.

For each pairing:
1. Headline font and body font, with the weights to use.
2. Why they work together (contrast, shared proportions, mood).
3. Sizes for web: H1, H2, body and caption in px, plus line height.
4. Where it could fail: tiny sizes, numbers, all caps, other languages, long paragraphs.
5. A sample: write a headline in my voice, a subhead and a 40-word body paragraph, and tell me how it should be set.

Rules: two fonts maximum per pairing. At least one pairing must be a single family using different weights. Avoid anything that screams template (no Lobster, no Papyrus, nothing that's on every coffee shop menu). Flag any license I need to check before using a font in a logo.
