Write 30 taglines for [brand]. What we do: [one line]. Why: [why statement]. Customer: [who]. Voice traits: [3 traits]. Things competitors already say: [list, so we avoid them].

Give me 5 in each of these styles:
1. Plain and true (says exactly what we do).
2. The belief (our why in a few words).
3. The challenge (an imperative, like "Be your own hero").
4. The wink (dry humor).
5. The contrast (us versus the usual way).
6. The weird one (might be terrible, might be genius).

Six words maximum each. Then pick your top 5 and test each one:
- Is it true?
- Could a competitor put it on their site tomorrow? If yes, kill it.
- Does it sound good said out loud?
- Does it still work in 10 years?
- Would my ideal customer screenshot it?
End with your single favorite and why.
