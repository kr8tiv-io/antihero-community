Help me build a quoting formula for [service]. Ask me one question at a time: my hourly costs (labour, equipment, vehicle), materials per [unit], travel, minimum job size, overhead, and the margin I want.
Then give me a simple formula, a worked example for a typical job, and a one-page quote template with scope, exclusions, timeline, deposit terms and expiry date. Use only my numbers and mark guesses clearly.
