title: "Service page that actually sells"
useItFor: "Write one service page from a short interview, in plain language."
category: Websites

---

Interview me about my [service] one question at a time (at least 8 questions): who buys it, the problem before, what the job involves, how long it takes, price range or how pricing works, what goes wrong with other providers, proof I have, and common objections.
Then write the page: a headline that names the result, a 2-sentence intro, How it works (3-5 steps), What's included, Who it's not for, FAQ (5 real objections), and one clear call to action. Use only facts I gave you. Mark anything missing as TODO instead of making it up.
