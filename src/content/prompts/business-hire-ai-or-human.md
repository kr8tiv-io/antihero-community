Here's a task that's eating my time: [describe it, how often, how long it takes, what goes wrong].
Tell me honestly if it's best handled by me, a part-time person, an existing software tool, or an AI agent. For each option give rough cost, setup time, what could go wrong, and how I'd check it's working. Then recommend one and give me the first 3 steps.
