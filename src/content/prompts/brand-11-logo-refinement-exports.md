Here's the logo concept I picked: [attach the image, or describe it]. My palette is [hex codes] and my headline font is [font].

1. Critique it like a design director: what's working, what's fussy, what will break at small sizes.
2. Suggest 3 specific refinements (spacing, weight, a detail to cut, a detail to add), and show the refined version if you can make images.
3. Run the tests: does it work at 16 and 32 px (favicon), in pure black, in pure white on dark, embroidered on a hat, as a sticker, squinting from across the room?
4. Write the usage rules: minimum size, clear space, what never to do (stretch, recolor, add shadows, put it on a busy photo).

If you can work with files: trace it to a clean SVG, then export full color on light, full color on dark, one-color black, one-color white, icon only, a 512 px square app icon and a 32 px favicon, as transparent PNGs at 4096, 1024 and 512 px. Name the files like [brand]-logo-[version]-[size].png and put them in one folder.
