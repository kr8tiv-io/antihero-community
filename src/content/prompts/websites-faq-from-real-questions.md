Here are real messages from customers: [paste emails, texts, DMs, comments].
Group them into the questions people actually keep asking. For each one, write a short, friendly answer (under 60 words) in my voice, using only what's true from my messages. Flag any question where I need to give you the real answer.
Then order the FAQ by how often it came up, and suggest which 3 answers should also go on the homepage.
