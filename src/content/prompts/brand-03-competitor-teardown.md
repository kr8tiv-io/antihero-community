Act as a ruthless but fair brand analyst. I'm building a brand for [your business] aimed at [your customer]. Here are [3 to 5] competitors or brands in my space:
1. [name] [website or social link]
2. [name] [link]
3. [name] [link]
[Optional: I've attached screenshots of their homepages, logos and recent posts.]

If you can browse, look at their websites, social pages and customer reviews. If you can't, work from the screenshots and tell me what you can't see.

For each competitor give me:
- Positioning in one sentence (what they want to be known for)
- Who they're really talking to
- Personality in 3 adjectives
- Visual identity: colors (hex guesses), type style, logo type, photo style
- Voice: 2 or 3 typical phrases they use
- What they do better than anyone
- Where they're generic, weak or forgettable
- What customers complain about

Then:
1. A comparison table across all of them.
2. The Sea of Sameness: the colors, words, photos and promises everyone uses. These are the things I should avoid.
3. Three white-space opportunities: positions nobody owns that fit me.
4. What I should steal (structure and ideas, never surface: no copying their logo, colors or slogans).
5. One sentence on how I could be the obvious choice for a specific customer they're all ignoring.
