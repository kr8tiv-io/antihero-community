Interview me about my business one question at a time (at least 8 questions): what we do, who we serve, 3 words customers use about us, competitors, what we never want to look like, and any colours or logos I already have.
Then give me a one-page brand kit: 5 colours with hex codes and where to use each, 2 free fonts (Google Fonts) with sizes for headings and body, voice in 5 do/don't pairs, 3 sample lines, and logo usage rules. Make it something I can hand to a designer or an AI.
