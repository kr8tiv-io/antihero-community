Act as a color strategist. Build my brand palette.

Brand: [name]. What we do: [one line]. Archetype: [archetype]. Personality: [3 traits]. Audience: [who]. Competitors' main colors: [list them, so we don't copy the category]. Colors I love: [list]. Colors I can't stand: [list]. Colors I have to keep: [list or none].

Give me 3 different palette options. For each one:
1. Primary, secondary, accent, plus a dark neutral and a light neutral, all with hex codes.
2. The 60-30-10 split: which color is the 60, the 30 and the 10.
3. The psychology reasoning for each color, with an honest note that color meaning depends on context, culture and shade.
4. Contrast check: give the contrast ratio for each text-on-background pair I'd actually use, and flag anything under 4.5:1 for body text or 3:1 for large headlines.
5. How it would look in real life: a website hero section, an Instagram post and a business card, described in two sentences each.

Then pick the one you'd choose for me and defend it in 3 sentences. Tell me what to avoid using these colors for.
