---
title: "The 5-second homepage test"
useItFor: "Find out if a stranger gets your homepage before they bounce."
category: Websites
goal: websites
order: 51
---

Act as a busy first-time visitor who has never heard of [business]. Here is my homepage: [URL or pasted copy/screenshot].
Look at it for 5 seconds, then answer: What do we sell? Who is it for? What should you click next? Then tell me what you would have guessed wrong.
After that, drop the act and give me: the 3 biggest points of confusion, a rewritten headline and subheadline (3 options each, under 12 words), and the one call to action the page should push. No new features, no redesign. Just clarity.
