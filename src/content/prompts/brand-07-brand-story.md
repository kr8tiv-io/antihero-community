Using my discovery brief and why statement below, write my brand story.

[paste your discovery brief and why statement]

Rules:
- The customer is the hero. I'm the guide who has been where they are. (Think Luke Skywalker and Yoda, not me in a cape.)
- Structure: the world as it is, the problem that annoyed me into action, the turning point, what I do now, what changes for the customer.
- Use my archetype: [archetype].
- Plain words. Real details. One specific moment beats ten adjectives.
- Banned: passionate, journey, innovative, solutions, world-class, unlock, elevate, seamless, and any sentence that could appear on a competitor's site.

Give me 3 lengths:
1. 25 words (social bio).
2. 100 words (About section, top of page).
3. 300 words (full About page), with a short headline.
Then give me a one-sentence version I can say out loud at a barbecue without sounding like a LinkedIn post.
