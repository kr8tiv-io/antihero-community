Write 15 subject lines for this email: [paste the email]. Honest, specific and interesting. No clickbait, nothing a stranger would report as spam, under [45] characters each.
Then pick the 3 most human ones, write the preview text for each, and tell me which one you would open and why. The test: would I open this if a stranger sent it?
