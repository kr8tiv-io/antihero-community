Plan my next 4 weeks of emails using give first: 3 emails of real value for every 1 ask.
My business: [what I sell] for [who].
What they care about: [list it, or paste your research].
The one thing I want to sell this month: [offer].
For each email give me the subject line, the gift at the top and 1 extra. For the ask email only, add a soft one-line ask at the bottom. No fake urgency, no countdown timers, no income claims.
