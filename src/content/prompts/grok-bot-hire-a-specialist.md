Create a new specialist Bot called [name]. Its job: [one job, for example research, SEO, bookkeeping or social posts].
Give it a short system prompt with:
- what it's responsible for
- the sources it should trust: [list]
- what it must never do without asking me: post, send, buy or delete anything
- how it reports back: short, with links
Show me the system prompt before you save it. One Bot, one job.
