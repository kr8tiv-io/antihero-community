Look through my sent email for quotes, proposals and offers from the last [30 days] that got no reply. For each one, tell me who, what, when I sent it, and the value if it's mentioned.
Then draft a friendly follow-up for each one that's older than [5 days]. Short, human, no guilt-tripping, and with one easy next step. Drafts only: I'll send them myself.
