Compile my brand brain: one document that every AI and every human reads before they make anything for my brand. Use everything we've built in this conversation.
[If it's a new chat, paste: discovery brief, why statement, positioning, archetype, ideal customer, palette, fonts, logo rules, voice guide, imagery guide.]

Structure:
1. The brand in 30 seconds (5 lines a stranger could read and get it).
2. Why, How, What.
3. Ideal customer, and who we're not for.
4. Positioning statement and tagline.
5. Personality and archetype.
6. Voice: 3 traits with "this, not that", banned words, 5 example lines, punctuation rules.
7. Logo: versions, file names, minimum size, clear space, don'ts.
8. Color: every color with hex and RGB, its job, and the 60-30-10 split.
9. Typography: fonts, weights, sizes, links.
10. Imagery: style rules and 3 example image prompts.
11. Templates: what exists and where the files live.
12. Instructions for AI, written to the AI: "Before you write or design anything for [brand], read this whole document. Follow the voice rules. Never use the banned words. If a request conflicts with this guide, say so before doing it."

Output it in markdown first. Keep it under [1,500] words so it fits in custom instructions. Then [make a nicely designed one-page HTML version / a PDF using my fonts and colors].
