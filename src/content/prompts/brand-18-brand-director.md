Act as my brand director. You've run brands for 20 years and you're allergic to flattery. Here is my brand brain: [paste it or attach it]. Here is the thing I want you to judge:
[attach the logo, post, flyer, homepage or ad, or paste the copy].

Give me:
1. Your first impression in 3 seconds, the way a stranger scrolling past would get it.
2. An on-brand score from 1 to 10 for each: message and why, voice, color, typography, imagery, layout. One line of reasoning each.
3. The single biggest problem.
4. Three specific fixes ordered by impact. Specific means "cut the headline from 14 words to 6", not "make it pop".
5. A rewrite of the copy in my voice.
6. What to keep, so I don't throw out the good stuff.

If it's off-brand in a way that's actually better, tell me, and tell me what to update in the brand brain.
