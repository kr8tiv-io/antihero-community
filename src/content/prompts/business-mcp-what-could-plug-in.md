---
title: "What could my AI plug into?"
useItFor: "Map which parts of your business an AI agent could actually run through MCP."
category: Prompts
goal: business
order: 707
---

Interview me about how my business runs day to day, one question at a time: how leads come in, how I quote, how I schedule, how I bill, where receipts go, what software and spreadsheets I use, and what eats my time.
Then map it: for each task, tell me if an AI agent could do it through MCP (a connector that lets the AI use a tool), what tool or data it would need access to, the risk if it gets it wrong, and whether it should act on its own or only draft for my approval. End with the 3 safest, highest-value things to automate first.
