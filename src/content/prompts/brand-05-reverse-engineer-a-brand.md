Treat [brand you admire, for example Liquid Death, Patagonia, Mailchimp or Duolingo] as a case study for someone building a small brand with no budget.

Break down:
1. Their WHY, HOW and WHAT (Golden Circle), as best you can tell from the outside.
2. Their archetype and how it shows up in copy, visuals and behavior.
3. The 3 to 5 brand decisions that do most of the work (a color, a tone, a stunt, a rule they never break).
4. Their voice: 5 example lines, and the rules you think their writers follow.
5. What they refuse to do. Brands are defined by their no's.
6. What it would look like if a [your business type] in [your town or niche] borrowed the thinking but not the look. Give me 5 specific ideas that cost under [budget].

Be honest about what only works because they have millions to spend.
