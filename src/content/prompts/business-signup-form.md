- Say exactly what people are signing up for: [one good email a week / a free guide / a checklist].
- No pre-checked boxes. Every email gets an easy unsubscribe link.
- I'm in [country], so follow [CAN-SPAM / CASL].
Tell me what I still need to check myself. This isn't legal advice, so flag anything a lawyer should look at.
