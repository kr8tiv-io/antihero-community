I have a meeting with [person/company] at [time] about [topic]. Pull everything you can find in my email, calendar and files about them, plus what's public online.
Give me: who they are in 2 lines, our history, what they probably want, what I want out of this, 3 smart questions to ask, and any landmines (old complaints, unpaid invoices, promises). One page, bullet points.
